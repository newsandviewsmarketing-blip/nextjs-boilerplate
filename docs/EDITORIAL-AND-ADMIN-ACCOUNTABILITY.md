# Editorial and administrator accountability

## Publication principle

User/company-submitted public records must not become public merely because a submitter saved them. Publication is controlled by a documented review workflow and server/database authorization.

## Role separation

- **Super Administrator:** users, roles, account status, final platform control, audit access and exceptional permanent deletion.
- **Verification Officer:** professional credentials, PVMC evidence and regulatory evidence; no routine product-publishing authority.
- **Marketplace & Content Admin:** product/content lifecycle and marketplace moderation; no user-role administration and no independent regulatory-verification authority.
- **Careers Admin:** job review and recruitment workflow; no product/user/credential authority.
- **Read-only Analyst:** reporting and audit visibility only.

## Mandatory moderation states

Use a controlled lifecycle such as:

`Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Changes Requested → Approved → Published → Suspended → Archived`

Products and jobs must not publish from company/user workspaces without the authorized administrative review path. Changes to sensitive verified fields should return the record to review.

## Editor sign-off

Before production promotion, the designated VetConnect editor/owner should verify:

- names, titles, company identities and public descriptions;
- factual dates, deadlines and locations;
- veterinary credential wording and verification badge wording;
- regulatory claims and the distinction between company-submitted data and independently reviewed evidence;
- product composition/strength/label text against the submitted source document where displayed;
- no private CV, certificate, registration evidence or internal notes are public;
- image ownership/permission and accurate alt text;
- spelling, English/Urdu presentation and mobile readability;
- SEO title/description does not contain private or unverified claims.

## Auditability

Every privileged publication/review action should record at minimum:

- actor/user ID;
- action;
- entity type and entity ID;
- decision/status;
- reason/internal note when applicable;
- timestamp;
- previous/new values where the workflow supports version history.

Routine administrative deletion should be archival/soft-delete. Permanent deletion should remain exceptional, Super-Administrator-only, deliberate and auditable.

## No self-verification

A company or professional may submit evidence, but must never assign itself a VetConnect verification badge or regulatory-verification status. Verification and publication are separate decisions where appropriate.
